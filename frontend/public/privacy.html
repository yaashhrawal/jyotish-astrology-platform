Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Grahika (“we”, “us”) provides Vedic astrology calculations and interpretation via our website and mobile app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short and honest.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email, and (if you set one) password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash; we never see them.
  • Birth details you enter — for each chart: name, date, time, and place of birth (city coordinates). These are needed to compute a horoscope.
  • Charts you save — stored to your account so they sync across your devices.
  • Basic technical logs — IP address and request time, used for security and rate-limiting.

You can use most calculations as a guest without an account. An account is needed only to save charts and use account features.

How we use it

  • To calculate and display charts, dashās, and interpretations.
  • To save and sync your charts across devices.
  • To secure the service (rate-limiting, abuse prevention).

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your birth details for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure needed to run the service (see below).

Third parties we rely on

  • Cloud hosting (Oracle Cloud) — stores our database and serves the app.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — used to look up city coordinates when you type a birth place.
  • Google Sign-In (optional) — if you choose it, Google shares your name and verified email so we can create your account.
  • AI interpretation — where an AI-written reading is offered, chart summary data may be sent to an AI provider to generate that text. No account credentials are sent.

Data storage & retention

Data is stored on encrypted-in-transit (HTTPS) connections in our cloud database, with daily encrypted backups. We keep your saved charts until you delete them or close your account.

Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your charts from within the app.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us — we will remove your account and saved data.

Children

Grahika is not directed at children under 13. Please do not create an account if you are under 13.

Astrology disclaimer

Grahika provides astrological information for personal insight and guidance only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice.
