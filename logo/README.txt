COLOURS
  Ink       #1C1917
  Saffron   #B4581F   (the light — never use it for the chart lines)
  Paper     #F7F3EC

TYPE (both SIL Open Font License — free to embed in the apps)
  Latin       EB Garamond, Medium 500, uppercase, +0.16em tracking
  Devanagari  Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Regular, +0.10em tracking

CLEAR SPACE
  Keep clear on all sides a margin equal to one small cell of the chart
  (1/4 of the mark's width). Nothing sits inside it.

MINIMUM SIZE
  Full mark        24 px / 8 mm
  Favicon variant  16 px  (use logo/svg/jyotish-favicon.svg below 24 px —
                    it has heavier strokes and drops the diagonals)

RIGHTS
The artwork is original and yours to use. The underlying diagram is the
traditional North Indian kundali — public domain, so nobody can restrict
your use of it, but also weakly distinctive on its own: if you register a
trade mark, register the combined mark (symbol + wordmark), not the chart
alone. This is practical guidance, not legal advice.
